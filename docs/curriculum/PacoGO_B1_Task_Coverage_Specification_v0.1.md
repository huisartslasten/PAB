Scope: B1 — Optellen  
Source basis:

## 1. Purpose and boundaries

This specification identifies practice-task coverage needed to operationalise the existing B1 model more broadly. It does not redesign the B1 skill, developments, claims, evidence targets, task models, mastery rules, or decision rules.

B1 remains: **Optellen** — combining two or more quantities/numbers and determining the total value.

The SLO mapping places whole and decimal numbers under KD10A and explicitly includes operations, choice of calculation form/strategy, mental calculation, estimation, written calculation, properties and procedures. These are not automatically separate skills. Strategy and method choice must first be handled within the existing relevant claims and cross-cutting mathematical thinking/work layers.

The supplied Basis workbook is only Theme 7 (printed pages 56–63). It is evidence for the contexts and operations in that theme, not evidence about the other Basis themes.

## 3. Current operational catalogue coverage

B1 Learner Task Catalogue v0.1 contains three tasks:
- B1-L01: combine two small quantity groups; D1/C1/TM1/ET1.
- B1-L02: symbolic addition 28 + 17; D2/C2/TM3/ET3.
- B1-L03: symbolic addition 46 + 28; D2/C2/TM3/ET3.

Observed catalogue gaps:
- D1 has one task and one small-quantity variation.
- D2 has two symbolic two-digit tasks, but no explicit task variation across written/contextual/decimal forms in this catalogue.
- D3 has no operational learner task.
- D4 has no operational learner task.
- ET2 (representation of the addition relation), ET4 (strategy), ET5 (checking), ET6 (robustness), ET7 (independence) and ET8 (transfer) are not explicitly covered by these three catalogue tasks.

This is a catalogue coverage statement, not a claim that the registry model lacks these targets.

## 5. Proposed task coverage within existing contracts

The following are candidate task families for a future catalogue revision. They are not yet approved executable tasks.

| Candidate family | Primary development / claim | Existing task model and evidence | Intended variation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quantity composition with varied group sizes | D1 / C1 | TM1 / ET1 | More than one small fixed example; quantities remain visibly interpretable |
| Represent an addition relation in more than one form | D2 / C2 | TM2 / ET1, ET2 | Quantity groups, equation, and/or number-line representation; preserve the same total |
| Symbolic addition with varied number size and regrouping demand | D2 / C2 | TM3 / ET3 | Whole numbers beyond the current two-digit examples; do not assume one written algorithm |
| Decimal addition in meaningful contexts | D2 / C2 | TM3 / ET3 | Decimal notation and money/measurement contexts; check place-value alignment in task design |
| Multi-addend money total / receipt | D2 or D4 depending on primary claim | TM3 for execution, TM6 only when transfer is the target | Multiple prices and totals; distinguish arithmetic performance from reading the receipt/data |
| Strategy selection for a suitable addition | D3 / C3 | TM4 / ET4 | Allow a suitable strategy choice; do not require written justification on every task |
| Estimate, calculate and check | D3 / C3 | TM4 / ET5 | Record estimate and checking only when explicitly required by the task |
| Apply addition under changed representation/context | D4 / C4 | TM5 / ET6 and/or TM6 / ET8 | Change surface form or context while retaining the addition relation; distinguish robustness from transfer |

### Coverage principle

A task should only request strategy selection, estimation, checking or explanation when that process is part of its declared evidence target. Do not force learners to justify every answer. Do not infer mastery from one correct answer.

A context task may involve reading data, money notation or measurement units. Those demands must not contaminate the interpretation of B1 evidence when the intended claim is addition. Task design should keep incidental reading/data demands proportionate and separately observable where necessary.

## 6. Candidate catalogue shape — not yet implemented

A future B1 catalogue revision should:
- retain B1-L01–L03 and their existing task identities/contracts unless a verified defect requires change;
- add tasks that explicitly cover D3 and D4;
- add D2 variation beyond the two existing two-digit symbolic tasks;
- explicitly cover ET2, ET4, ET5, ET6, ET7 and ET8 across the catalogue;
- use distinct variation keys for materially different number forms, representations and contexts;
- avoid duplicating many near-identical arithmetic items as if they were independent evidence;
- separate practice support/prompting from assessment evidence and record independence only where the task contract calls for it;
- keep task-specific answers and scoring separate from mastery decisions.

No fixed total task count is prescribed here. The final count must follow the evidence coverage and variation design, not a target number.

## 7. Interfaces and instructional method

Current A1 content names reusable Number Line and Quantity/Number Representation interfaces. These may support B1 where pedagogically appropriate.

A written-calculation/place-value interface is only a future candidate. It must not be built before:
1. the school's actual instructional method is confirmed from a reliable source;
2. the interface requirements are separated from method-specific instruction;
3. alternative methods/notations can be supported without changing the curriculum claim;
4. interaction, state/evidence and rendering remain separate.

When the school method is unknown, PacoGO must not present an invented procedure as the classroom method. Conceptual support may be used without claiming method alignment.

## 8. Registry/catalogue version integration gate

The current B1 learner catalogue v0.1 declares `curriculum_registry_version: 0.4.0-candidate`, while the current repository registry is `0.6.0-candidate`.

The B1 skill object was compared directly between registry 0.4 and 0.6. Its B1 definition, four developments, four claims, eight evidence targets, six task models, development rules, evidence rule, mastery rules and decision rules are identical in the two files. This confirms that the B1 model itself did not change across these registry versions.

However, the B1 learner route explicitly fetches `PacoGO_Curriculum_Registry_v0.4.json` and looks up the registered version using the catalogue's `0.4.0-candidate` value. The registry version is also passed to learner-history loading and persistence. Therefore the version pointer is not merely decorative metadata: it participates in runtime selection and learner-state/history scoping.

Do not update this pointer mechanically. Before a catalogue revision or learner-route integration:
- inspect TEST registration/read-back for registry 0.6;
- inspect existing B1 persisted history/evidence by registry version;
- determine whether switching the route to 0.6 would split or strand prior history;
- decide whether compatibility requires preserving the v0.4 route or migrating/reconciling state through an explicit designed path;
- update the route, catalogue and tests together only after the persistence/runtime contract is understood.

No Supabase change is implied by this specification.

## 9. Acceptance criteria for the next design step

Before authoring executable new tasks, produce a traceability table where every proposed task has:
- one primary development and claim;
- one or more existing evidence targets and task models;
- an explicit response and observation contract;
- a meaningful variation key;
- a clear distinction between target skill and incidental context demand;
- a statement of whether strategy, estimate, checking or explanation is actually required;
- a rationale grounded in the supplied workbooks or curriculum model.

The design must demonstrate coverage of D3 and D4 and the currently uncovered evidence targets without changing the underlying B1 claims or mastery rules.

## 10. Non-goals and epistemic status

This document does not:
- add or rename skills, developments, claims, evidence targets or task models;
- change mastery or next-action rules;
- establish a fixed developmental sequence empirically;
- claim that workbook coverage proves learner mastery;
- prescribe a single written algorithm;
- create learner data or change Supabase;
- implement or deploy new learner tasks.

Status remains candidate design. Empirical validation requires separate learner-data research and validation work.
