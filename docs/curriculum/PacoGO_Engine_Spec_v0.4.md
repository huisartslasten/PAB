# PacoGO Engine Specification v0.4

Status: candidate technical specification
Datum: 2026-11-02

## 1. Scope

v0.4 extends v0.3 with:

1. Explicit alternative-explanation representation.
2. Explicit uncertainty metadata.
3. Non-empirical validation hooks C1-C10.
4. Persistent schema support for the new metadata.

The engine remains generic. A1, B1 and FR2 are representative configurations, not hard-coded cases.

## 2. Decision contract

`EVIDENCE -> INFERENCE -> MASTERY -> DECISION -> NEXT ACTION`

The exact curriculum registry version remains part of provenance.

## 3. Alternative explanations

An observation may carry one or more competing explanations. These are propagated into longitudinal state and decision trace.

The presence of an alternative explanation does not establish that the curriculum explanation is wrong. It means the current evidence is compatible with more than one interpretation and may require targeted evidence.

The runtime therefore records alternatives rather than assigning probabilities without an empirical basis.

## 4. Uncertainty

Current uncertainty is represented structurally through:
- unresolved developments;
- contradiction count and identifiers;
- missing evidence targets;
- alternative explanation count and content;
- validation-hook status.

No probability or confidence percentage is generated by the prototype unless a future empirically justified model explicitly supplies one.

## 5. Validation hooks C1-C10

The runtime exposes ten named validation-hook slots: C1 through C10.

A hook has status `NOT_RUN` by default. Optional configuration may attach a scope description. Runtime execution of a hook is not itself empirical evidence and never changes a registry from Candidate to Supported or Validated.

The meanings and acceptance criteria of C1-C10 must be defined by the empirical validation protocol before any hook can be interpreted as a scientific result.

## 6. Persistence

`student_longitudinal_state` stores the current derived state, including alternative explanations and validation-hook metadata.

`curriculum_decision_trace` stores the historical decision trace, including alternative explanations and validation-hook metadata.

Both remain linked to the exact `curriculum_registry_versions` record.

## 7. Scientific boundary

Candidate remains the status of the current curriculum seed and all current mastery/development rules.

Software execution, traceability, contradiction handling and validation-hook availability establish technical auditability only. They do not establish construct validity, developmental validity, mastery cut-score validity, instructional effectiveness or generalizability.

A future empirical workflow may promote a narrowly scoped claim from Candidate to Supported and, only after the project's explicit stronger criteria are met, to Validated.
