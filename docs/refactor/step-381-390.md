## Source-proven validation contract
The authoritative V4.78 `saveLesson()` performs its required-field check after `collectItems()` and before any dictation spelling check or persistence.

The condition is equivalent to:

- student must be present;
- subject must be present;
- title must be present;
- collected items must be a non-empty collection.

When the condition fails, V4.78 sets the editor error to exactly:

`Vul het vak, de lestitel en minstens één item in.`

The validation boundary does not inspect the contents of individual items. Item-shape validation belongs to the collection boundary, not this gate.
